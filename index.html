About Harvest Hope

🎯 Educational Purpose

Harvest Hope teaches the complexities of addressing global food insecurity. Based on real research and interventions proven to work.

📚 Real Solutions

Every intervention in the game is based on evidence-based solutions: cash transfers, climate-smart agriculture, AI technology, and more.

🌱 Impact-Driven

Learn how decisions cascade through systems. See how successful interventions spread to neighboring regions.

🔗 UN SDG 2

Aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goal 2: Zero Hunger. Help achieve food security and improved nutrition by 2030.

Research & References

  • Drought-Resistant Crops: Pearl millet yields increase 55% in dry conditions
  • AI Crop Detection: Nuru app in Kenya increased yields by 30% ($300/year per farmer)
  • School Feeding: Improves enrollment and nutrition outcomes
  • Irrigation: Reduces childhood stunting by 20% in Sub-Saharan Africa
