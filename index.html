Mission

About Harvest Hope

🎯 Educational Purpose

Harvest Hope teaches the real-world complexities of addressing global food insecurity, grounded in actual research and proven interventions.

📚 Evidence-Based

Every intervention is drawn from real-world programs: cash transfers, climate-smart agriculture, AI-assisted crop detection, and more.

🌱 Systems Thinking

See how decisions cascade. Watch successful programs spread to neighboring regions and understand the interconnected nature of hunger.

🔗 UN SDG 2

Aligned with the UN's Zero Hunger goal — SDG 2. Help players understand what it actually takes to achieve food security by 2030.

Research & References

  • Cash Transfers: Kenya's Hunger Safety Net Programme shows 15% increase in household dietary diversity
  • Drought-Resistant Crops: Pearl millet yields increase 55% in dry conditions vs. conventional varieties
  • AI Crop Detection: Nuru app in Kenya increased yields by 30%, worth $300/year per farmer
  • School Feeding: Improves both enrollment rates and long-term nutrition outcomes
  • Irrigation: Small-scale irrigation reduces childhood stunting by 20% in Sub-Saharan Africa
